What to visit

The area & trip ideas

Slatiňany lies in a picturesque landscape on the Chrudimka River, at the edge of the Iron Mountains. Nearby you'll find castles, nature and historic towns — ideal for walking and cycling trips.

In Slatiňany

Slatiňany Chateau

A romantic chateau with a large park and a unique Hippological Museum devoted to horses. A pleasant walk for families with children too.

zamek-slatinany.cz →
In Slatiňany

National Stud — Slatiňany

Breeding of the noble black Kladruby horses. You can watch the horses in the pastures and stroll through the surrounding countryside.

nhkladruby.cz →
≈ 3 km

Švýcárna & game park

A wooded hill with a game park and an interactive Museum of the Kladruby Horse. A popular destination for walks and views over the region.

More about Švýcárna →
≈ 3 km

Kočičí hrádek (Cat's Castle)

A charming romantic folly hidden in the woods above Slatiňany. A popular goal for a short walk through the forest.

≈ 4 km · Chrudim

Museum of Puppet Cultures

The historic town of Chrudim with a unique puppet museum, a town square and pleasant cafés. A great destination even in bad weather.

puppets.cz →
≈ 5 km · Podhůra

Bára Lookout Tower

An unusual wooden lookout tower in the Podhůra recreational forest near Chrudim. Views over the region, refreshments and trails for families and cyclists.

lesychrudim.cz →
≈ 12 km · Pardubice

Pardubice — castle & gingerbread

A regional city with a Renaissance chateau, a historic centre, gingerbread and its famous steeplechase. Ideal for a full-day trip.

Pardubice Chateau · vcm.cz →
≈ 18 km · near Pardubice

Kunětická Hora Castle

A majestic castle on a solitary hill above the Elbe lowlands. Tours, far-reaching views and a rich programme of cultural events all season long.

hrad-kunetickahora.cz →
≈ 15 km · Miřetice

Ležáky Memorial

Memorial grounds commemorating the hamlet destroyed by the Nazis in 1942. A museum with a documentary and freely accessible grounds in a quiet valley.

lezaky-memorial.cz →
Nasavrky

Land of the Celts

A Celtic open-air archaeological park in Nasavrky bringing the life of the ancient Celts to life. A popular family trip with a programme for children and adults.

More at zemekeltu.cz →
Within reach

Železné hory (Iron Mountains)

A protected landscape area full of hiking trails, viewpoints and cycle paths. Nature right on the apartment's doorstep.

geoparkzh.cz →
≈ 20 km · Iron Mountains

Seč Reservoir & Oheb Castle

A popular reservoir on the Chrudimka for swimming and boat trips. Above the water rise the romantic ruins of Oheb Castle with a wonderful view.

More about Oheb & the reservoir →
≈ 22 km · Třemošnice

Lichnice Castle Ruins

Extensive Gothic ruins on a promontory above Třemošnice, known as the "gate to the Iron Mountains". A new lookout tower offers panoramic views.

More about Lichnice →
≈ 20 km · Hlinsko area

Veselý Kopec & Betlém Hlinsko

The Vysočina open-air museum — a collection of folk buildings and crafts. Betlém in Hlinsko also features an exhibition on Shrovetide, inscribed on the UNESCO list.

vesely-kopec.eu →
≈ 18 km · Chrudim–Hlinsko area

Trhová Kamenice

A picturesque little town in the Chrudimka valley with a Classicist square on the border of the Iron Mountains and the Žďár Hills. A starting point for the local ponds and the lookout tower on Zuberský Hill.

More about Trhová Kamenice →
Along the Chrudimka

Cycling & walking

Quiet routes along the Chrudimka River connecting Slatiňany, Chrudim and the surrounding villages. Suitable for families and easygoing cycling.
